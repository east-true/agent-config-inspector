# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Examples of positive behavior include giving useful technical feedback, assuming good intent, respecting different experience levels, and focusing criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through the project's available GitHub contact channels. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other material, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Attribution

This policy is intentionally concise and follows the common principles used by open-source community codes of conduct.
